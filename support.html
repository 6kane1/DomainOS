How it works

01A form and a "before" report

After booking you fill in a short form (hardware, games, what bothers you) and run our one-command report. Photos of the BIOS screens if we tune the BIOS.

02A personal plan

We read the report and send a plan of 5–8 points: what we change, why, and how to undo it. We start only after your "yes".

03The session

Discord voice and remote access (RustDesk or AnyDesk) only with your permission. Everything we change is written down; your personal files are not opened.

04A before / after report

The same day: a table of FPS, 1% and 0.1% low and stutters in the same scene, the list of changes and how to undo each. Then check-ins during the support month.

FAQ

What if it does not get better?

Every change can be undone, we show how. If you are not happy with the result, a second session is free: we find out why.

Is it safe?

We never touch Windows security. In the BIOS you change values yourself with our guidance, after saving your own profile. A short stability test after each block.

Do I need Domain OS first?

No. If needed we install it during the session by our guide, on a clean Windows 11 Pro or over the current one.

How do I pay?
