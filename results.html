02▍Weekly top 5

Which results stay on the website

Every result of the current week (Monday–Sunday, Moscow time) shows up here right away. When the week is over, the bot keeps its five most interesting results on the website; the rest stay on Discord. The bot posts each week’s top in the "Weekly top 5" thread of the results channel.

01

A clear difference. What counts most is how far a variant is from the first group (the base) in the key measurements: 1% and 0.1% low, frame time, hitches, waits for a core. Improvements and losses both count. Under 2% is noise and does not count.

02

More runs, more trust. Every run (up to five) adds 10% to the score; a real comparison of two or more variants is worth more than a single one.

03

Different hardware. When two results are close, a processor that is not in the week’s top yet goes first.
